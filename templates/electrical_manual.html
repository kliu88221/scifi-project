ELECTRICAL SYSTEM MANUAL

WARNING:
Improper activation sequencing may result in system-wide power surges and loss of onboard records.
Proceed with caution.

PRIMARY POWER LINES

The fuse box contains 6 power lines:

  • Purple
  • Blue
  • Green
  • Yellow
  • Orange
  • Red

SEQUENCING CONSTRAINTS

  • Purple must be activated before Blue
  • Green must be activated before Blue
  • Orange must be activated after Green
  • Green must be activated before Yellow
  • Red must be activated after Purple but before Green
  • Yellow cannot be activated before Blue
  • Blue must be activated second to last
